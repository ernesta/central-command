Training entry: the side panels

Three ways to lay out Task, Outline and Files. In all three: the outline sits right next to the text, Files is the far-right column, and every panel's heading row has the same height (so FILES no longer sits lower than OUTLINE). Shown in your system's light or dark mode.

Same in all three: the alignment fix. Files' three icon buttons are 28px tall and push its heading row below the Outline's text-only heading. The shared Panel gets a fixed 28px minimum heading row, so a label sits at the same height in every panel, in every module.
A Recommended

Task is a wide strip under the metadata

One quiet line: what the task is, its total, and the two actions. The side column is then only two reference panels.
