SSSR 2027 Updates: Abstract Submissions Are Now Open

SSSR 2027 Submit Your Abstract Today

The SSSR Thirty-Fourth Annual Conference (SSSR 2027) will take place at Le Meridien Santiago in Santiago, Chile, from July 14–17, 2027. We look forward to welcoming members and colleagues from around the world for this annual gathering of the Society for the Scientific Study of Reading.

The views and opinions expressed by conference presenters and participants are their own and do not necessarily reflect those of the Society for the Scientific Study of Reading.
