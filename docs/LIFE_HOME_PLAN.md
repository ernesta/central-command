Planned 28 Oct 2026 (the user's request). **All five stages built, tested and driven 28 Oct 2026 (see "Status" below and `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Home: Life has one" and "Watch: sources per workspace").** This reverses, on purpose, "Home: Life is left out of the summary" (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, 28 Oct 2026). ## 1. What Life's Home shows

Same page, same components as Research's and Work's (`HomePage`, `TodayCard`, `ComingUpCard`, `HomeHours`), a Life landing with the date heading and the cards above the module cards. Only what Life has:

- **Today**: Life's tasks overdue or due today, then today's timed Life trainings (`Training · Online`). A Life self-study task is one row named as its training, as in Research.
- **Coming up**: the next 3 days of Life tasks and trainings.
- **Hours**: Life's plan has no aim (`LIFE_PLAN.hoursPerWeek` is 0), so the card is the "This week" total with "Today …" under it and no progress bar, no clients. `HomeHours` already has that branch (`hasAim(data) && aim > 0` else a plain total); stage 2 only checks that `periodFigures` gives "This week" for Life (it has `weekAim: true`, so it asks for an invoice period; `invoiceOf` must say week). **Life's hours count as Research's and Work's do** (the user, 28 Oct 2026): task time plus the hours its notes derive. Today that is Research only (`useMeetingEntries` reads trainings for Research alone, `shownYearOf` in `collect.ts` too, and Life's meetings are skipped), so a Life training's own session is missing from Life's Hours. Stage 2 makes Life read its trainings in both places, so the Hours page, Home, the summary and `snapshot.json` agree (`docs/HOURS_CONSISTENCY_PLAN.md`). Life has no meetings for now (the user); the same lists feed the derived hours, so they count when Life gets meetings.
- **The running timer**: not a card (as everywhere, it is in the top bar). It reaches the summary for Life (see 2).
- **Left out**: Meetings and Time off (the user: not wanted for now), so no "Next meeting" and no days off in Coming up; Readings. **Watch for Life** is wanted: sources are separate per workspace (stage 4). The heading's weather is workspace-independent and shows as in Research and Work.
