EPS Research Workshop: Birmingham City University. 20th January 2027.

The Science of Learning: Integrating Experimental Psychology into Higher Education Research.
Organised by Katy Burgess.

Research into higher education teaching and learning has expanded rapidly (Bull et al., 2024), yet much of this work relies on qualitative, single-institution case-studies (Crawford, 2025). Consequently, it is often difficult to determine why pedagogical interventions are effective, due to the absence of appropriate control groups, valid and reliable measurements, and/or statistical analyses (Taylor et al., 2025). Greater integration of experimental psychological theories and methods is therefore needed to enable rigorous evaluation of what works, for whom, and why, in teaching, assessment, and student support.

This workshop addresses this issue by highlighting the contribution that experimental psychology can make to pedagogical research and strengthening methodological skills. Attendees will engage with a series of experimental research talks, alongside opportunities to consolidate learning and develop collaborative research projects.
