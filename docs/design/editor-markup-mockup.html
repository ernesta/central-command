Notes editor: three ways to make markup visible and editing predictable

Shown on the same small note. 1 is the fix for the jumping; 2 and 3 are about seeing the markup. They stack: 1 is needed under both.

1. Fix the jump: Backspace undoes the auto-format

Typing ### still makes a heading. Backspace straight afterwards gives the literal ### back, and the cursor stays where it was. Backspace at the start of a heading (with text) turns it into a paragraph in place, without merging upward.

2. Source mode: switch to the raw Markdown

A switch in the card’s footer (or ⌘/) flips the same note between the rendered view and the raw text in a monospaced editor. What you type in source mode is saved exactly as typed.

3. Live markup on the line you are editing

Everything looks rendered except the block or word under the cursor, which shows its markup (like Obsidian’s Live Preview). Move away and it turns back into formatting. No mode to switch, and you can always see and edit the markup.
