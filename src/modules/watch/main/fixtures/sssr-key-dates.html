Registration & Key Dates

SSSR 2027 Annual Conference 

Santiago, Chile 
July 14–17, 2027
 
SSSR's Thirty-Fourth Annual Conference will be held in-person at Le Meridien Santiago, Santiago, Chile. Key dates, registration details, and submission information are listed below. All abstract submissions are managed through Ex Ordo, SSSR's official conference platform.
 

Key Dates

Milestone Date
First Call for Papers Monday, September 14, 2026
Abstract Submissions Open Monday, September 28, 2026
Proposal Submission Deadline Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Conference Decision Notifications Wednesday, January 20, 2027

Registration Opens Monday, January 25, 2027
Early-Bird, Presenting Author & Discussant Registration Deadline Friday, April 16, 2027
Final Scheduling Requests Deadline Friday, April 30, 2027

Annual Conference — Santiago, Chile July 14–17, 2027

All deadlines end at 11:59 PM Pacific Time, North America. Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances.

Proposal Submissions

The submission portal is managed through Ex Ordo and will open on September 28, 2026. The submission link will be shared via the SSSR listserv and posted here once the portal is live. Submissions that fail to follow posted guidelines may not be reviewed. 

Portal: sssr2027.exordo.com (available from September 28, 2026)

Registration

Full conference overview will be posted here before registration opens on January 25, 2027. Presenting authors and discussants must register by April 16, 2027, to secure their place in the programme. Failure to register by this deadline will result in the automatic removal of a submission from the programme.

Questions? Contact the SSSR Association Manager at scientificstudy.reading@gmail.com
