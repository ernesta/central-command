3 · Rules the backend already follows
Nothing is shown while the master switch is off.
The digest waits for its time and is skipped on an empty day.
A meeting reminder comes once, between "N minutes before" and the start.
A grouped Watch notice covers every item in it, so the next one is only about newer items.
A deadline notice comes once per deadline, from N days before.
"Already told" is kept in a small file in the data folder and forgotten after 30 days.

Open question for you: quiet hours (nothing between 18:00 and 08:00, or at weekends). Not built; the digest time covers mornings only.
