Subtask rows

Same three tasks as your screenshot. The ugly part is that a subtask row is mostly empty cells (List, Priority and Time are blank) in a table built for full tasks. The options differ in what a subtask row says and whether it keeps the columns. In all of them a subtask has no priority of its own: it takes its parent's (as a due date is copied when a subtask is made), so the Priority control and the stored value go away.

Option C

Subtasks are a thread, only title and date

The subtask drops the columns it cannot use: its title runs across List, Priority and Time, and only the date stays in the Due column. A thread line ties it to the parent. Nothing blank, nothing repeated. List and priority are read from the parent row directly above.

Option D

Subtasks inside the parent's row

No rows of their own: the pill opens a small checklist inside the parent's Task cell, each line with its date at the right edge. The parent row just grows. Compact and clearly one unit, but you can no longer tab or right-click a subtask as a row, and long lists make one tall row.
